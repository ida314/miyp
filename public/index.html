How it works

1
Share your struggle

Describe what's troubling you in plain language. No Buddhist vocabulary required.

2
Teachings are retrieved

Relevant suttas are matched by problem category and semantic similarity, not keyword search.

3
Grounded guidance

A practical response is offered in modern language — with citations to the original texts.

"Do not accept anything by mere tradition, or because it has been said by your teacher. When you know for yourselves: these things are wholesome, these things are blameless — then you should accept them and practice them."
— The Kālāma Sutta, AN 3.65

Eight spokes. Eight struggles.

The dharma wheel's eight spokes correspond to the eight problem categories the system addresses.

🌊
Anxiety & Fear
Worry, dread, nervous anticipation of what might go wrong
 🔥
Anger & Aversion
Irritation, resentment, ill will toward people or situations
 🌙
Craving & Attachment
Clinging, compulsion, wanting more of what you already have
 🍂
Grief & Loss
Bereavement, mourning change, sorrow over what is gone
 💨
Restlessness
Scattered attention, inability to settle, constant seeking
 ⚖️
Ethical Conflict
Guilt, moral uncertainty, doing right when it costs you
 🪞
Doubt & Indecision
Paralysis, second-guessing, inability to commit to a path
 🗣️
Speech & Relationships
Conflict, difficult conversations, how we speak and listen
